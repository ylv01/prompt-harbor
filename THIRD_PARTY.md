# Third-party sources and attribution

Source URLs, titles, publishers, dates and original concise factual summaries
are recorded in `skills/promptharbor/data/sources.json` and `evidence.json`.
Community origins, author names, report artifacts and editorial assessments are
recorded with their evidence; `community_research.json` records each model's
searches. The generated [community table](https://github.com/ylv01/prompt-harbor/blob/main/docs/COMMUNITY.md) presents those source
links and task-specific judgments for review.
No model weights, full articles, source screenshots or benchmark datasets are
included. The project's Apache-2.0 license covers original curation and code;
it does not relicense upstream materials or grant model usage rights.

Independent measurements used here are attributed to **Artificial Analysis**,
**Vals AI**, and **Arena**. Their publications, data services, APIs and datasets retain their
own terms. Epoch AI is referenced for methodology, not a copied result table.
Official model documentation and model cards belong to their respective
publishers. Hugging Face hosting does not make a vendor claim independent.

Community summaries credit their originating authors and teams through the
source directory and evidence records, including public blogs, firsthand forum
posts, GitHub reports and linked demonstrations. Our 0–10 task assessments are
PromptHarbor's editorial judgments, not scores attributed to those authors unless
an exact source measurement is separately identified. Linked demos remain on
their authors' sites; their code and images are not redistributed or claimed as
our own tests. A documented independent rerun is identified explicitly when one
exists; merely reading a report does not establish reproduction.

The which-llm, RouteLLM and RouterBench links acknowledge related work. Their
code was not incorporated. All logos and badges in this repository were created
for PromptHarbor; no provider logo, stock image or template was reused.

Brand PNGs may be regenerated using Pillow and system fonts. The repository
does not redistribute font files. Model/provider names remain their owners'
marks. Inclusion is not endorsement or a claim of affiliation.
